What it does

  • BPM and key: read for every track and written into the tags (where DJ apps read them), and into the file name if you want.
  • Exact BPM: round a track to a whole tempo, such as 127.8 to 128, without changing its key.
  • Loudness: even tracks out for streaming (−14 LUFS) or for the club (−9 LUFS), so one doesn't jump out louder than the next.
  • Extended mix: add 16 or 32 bars of intro and outro, cut from the track itself on the beat, so it's easy to mix in and out.
  • Convert: MP3 up to 320 kbps or WAV, sample rate, bit depth, stereo or mono.
  • Tidy: titles, artist and cover for every file, silence trimmed at the start and end, tracks numbered in your order. Download them all at once as a ZIP.

Why it's different

Most online converters upload your music to their server. DriftMix does the work in your browser, so unreleased tracks stay yours. There's no account and no cookies, and the code is open for anyone to check.
