Privacy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Your audio files never leave your browser. DriftMix reads, mixes, converts and records everything on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is kept on our side, and nothing is used to train anything.

There are no accounts, no cookies, no ads, and we don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your device

  • Your tracks. Read by your browser when you load them. Your Files list, Auto DJ playlist and sample pads are kept in your browser's own storage, so they're still there after a refresh. Clear removes them, and so does clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • Recordings. Vocal Booth takes and recorded sets are made on your device. They become files only when you press download or share.
  • Settings and hot cues. Kept in your browser's storage on this device.
  • The app itself. Its files are saved in your browser so DriftMix opens fast and works offline.

Microphone

The Vocal Booth uses your microphone only after you press Record, and your browser asks you first. The sound stays on your device.

Demo tracks

When you press the demo button, three tracks by The Drift3r are downloaded from driftmix.app to your browser and played there.

Visitor numbers

We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits (for example, how many people opened DriftMix and from which country). It does not use cookies or local storage and does not build a profile of you. We only see totals. It never sees your audio.

Hosting

DriftMix is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the app and protect it from attacks. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Check it yourself

DriftMix is open source: read the code. The site's security rules also stop the page from sending anything anywhere except driftmix.app itself and Cloudflare's visit counter.

Why we process this data

Counting visits and keeping the app secure are our legitimate interests under UK data protection law (UK GDPR). We keep only what is needed for that.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Write to the contact above. If you're unhappy with how we handle it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
