About DriftMix

DriftMix by The Drift3r is a free DJ app that runs in your browser. Load two tracks: it tells you if they'll mix, then mixes them on the beat. Nothing uploaded, no sign-up.

Open DriftMix →

What it does

  • Decks: two decks that read BPM and Camelot key, with a match light that says honestly if two tracks will mix, and what fits next from your own tracks.
  • Transitions: one press for a beat-matched blend (8, 16 or 32 bars, with a bass swap), an echo-out or a build-up, with a line after each saying how it was done.
  • Auto DJ: put your tracks in order and press Start. Set types for a warm-up, the gym, peak time or a chilled dinner; record the set as MP3 with a tracklist and a mix card to post.
  • Samples and Vocal Booth: loops cut on the beat, pads, and vocals recorded in time over a beat.
  • Files: BPM and key written into tags, loudness for streaming or club, extended DJ intros and outros, MP3 or WAV.

Questions

Is driftmix.app the DriftMix ambient sound app for iPhone?
No. That's a different app by a different developer, for mixing ambient sounds such as rain and waves. DriftMix by The Drift3r is a DJ app for mixing music tracks, and it runs in the browser on any device.

Do I need to install anything or sign up?
No. Open driftmix.app in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. You can also install it as an app from the browser, and it works offline. There's no account.

Are my tracks uploaded?
No. Everything happens on your own device. The code is open source (AGPL-3.0), so anyone can check it. More on the privacy page.

Who makes DriftMix?
The Drift3r, a London DJ and artist (thedrift3r.com), under DanceOnVibes Limited.
