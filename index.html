DriftMix by The Drift3r

A free DJ app that runs in your browser. Load two tracks: DriftMix tells you if they'll mix, then mixes them on the beat. Nothing uploaded, no sign-up.

  • Two decks with BPM, Camelot key and a match light
  • One-press transitions: blend, echo-out and build-up
  • Auto DJ with set types, set recording and a shareable mix card
  • Samples, Vocal Booth and Files (BPM and key tags, loudness, extended mixes)
